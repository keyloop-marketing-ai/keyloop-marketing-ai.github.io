Keyloop Marketing AI

An internal assistant for Keyloop's marketing team

Keyloop Marketing AI is an extension for Claude Desktop, used by Keyloop's marketing team in Canada and the United Kingdom to manage the digital marketing of the car dealerships Keyloop works for. It lets team members ask Claude, in plain English, for reports and changes across the advertising and analytics accounts they already manage.

What it does

  • Reads performance data from the dealers' Google Ads, Google Analytics 4 and Google Search Console accounts, and from Meta Ads, SEMrush, Promptwatch and Microsoft Clarity.
  • Builds monthly cross-channel reports for each dealer.
  • Makes changes the team member asks for (for example, pausing a campaign or adjusting a budget), records them, and can undo most of them.

Who can use it

Only members of Keyloop's marketing team, signing in with Google accounts that already have access to the dealers' accounts. It is not offered to the public. The extension can only reach accounts the signed-in Google login can already access.

Google data

When a team member signs in with Google, the extension asks for access to Google Ads, Google Analytics and Google Search Console, plus access to Google Cloud to read the team's shared settings. How that data is used is described in the Privacy Policy.
